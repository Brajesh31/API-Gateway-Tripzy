# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want API Gateway Tripzy to remain a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous project space. Contributors may have different backgrounds, identities, skill levels, and professional experience. Participation should be safe and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Give actionable feedback on code, tests, and documentation.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct inaccurate claims.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Credit contributions and respect intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following conduct is not acceptable:
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Publishing credentials, customer data, or private service details.
- Deliberately exposing others to security or privacy risk.
- Spam, impersonation, or knowingly disruptive behavior.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical criticism is welcome when it is specific and respectful. Challenge the implementation, assumptions, test evidence, and trade-offs. Do not attack the contributor. Security concerns should be raised promptly and through the appropriate private channel when public disclosure would create risk.

## Reporting a concern

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting path in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

Include enough context to understand the behavior, but do not forward private information or credentials unnecessarily.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project spaces. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and handled consistently.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, project-related communications, and any space where a person is representing the project.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open-source community conduct practices. It is maintained as a project policy and may be revised as the contributor community grows.
